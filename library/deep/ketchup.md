# ketchup

## When to Load
- The user returns after a long run, or asks "what happened" or "what do you need from me".
- Closing the answer of any route, or the user asks for the status.

## Pattern
1. Stay read-only. Gather your work, worker reports, and other messages since the user's last message.
2. Open with one sentence: what waits on the user, or that nothing does.
3. Say what changed for the user in plain words, under 100 words. Skip hashes, paths, and counts unless a decision needs one.
4. Give one short bullet per request: done, running, failed, or not checked.
5. List every call only the user can make: a product choice, approval, irreversible step, or missing access.
6. For each call give: the link or id, what it changes for users, the evidence, the risk, and your pick.
7. For a choice between options, list each option with its gain and its cost.
8. When several items share one call, state the call and your pick once, then list items riskiest first.
9. Write "unknown" for a fact you lack. Never invent a call to fill the reply.
10. Show a screenshot or diagram when it explains faster than words.

## Red Flags
- Narrating process instead of outcomes.
- A decision listed without a recommendation.
